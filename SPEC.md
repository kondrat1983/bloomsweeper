## 1. The game in short

Read `docs/gdd/GDD.md` for the full design. The loop: **Find** creatures hidden in the drawing,
each found creature switches on its clue; **Deduce** which cells are BLOOM and which are GREEN,
by logic only; **Paint** the solved cells in your own palette. Some creatures are only visible once
the cells around them are painted, so painting feeds the search. No fail state: a wrong mark leaves
an ink blot that can be washed away.

## 2. Stack (proposed, confirm in DECISIONS)

- **Vanilla JS, ES modules, SVG** for the page. Each logic cell is one SVG `<path>`; painting
  changes its fill. No game engine: vector line art, hit-testing and zoom are what SVG does best.
- **Pan and zoom:** a small vendored library (for example `@panzoom/panzoom`) or own code. Must
  feel smooth with pinch on iPhone and iPad.
- **No build step if possible.** If one is needed, keep it to a single dev dependency (Vite) and
  commit nothing generated except `dist/` on the Pages branch.
- **PWA:** installable, offline after first load, service-worker cache name bumped with every
  version.
- **Tools in Node** (`tools/`): adjacency extraction, solver, generator, validator, preview render.
  Rasterisation and screenshots through Playwright with the preinstalled Chromium.
- **Audio:** small synthesized Web Audio effects (pencil scratch, page turn, soft chime). No audio
  files with unclear rights.
- **Tests:** Node built-in test runner for tools and rules, Playwright for the app. CI on GitHub
  Actions for every PR.

## 3. Page format

A page is two files in `pages/<chapter>/<id>/`:

- `page.svg`: the drawing. Logic cells are `<path id="c001" data-kind="flower|leaf">`.
  Decoration is in `<g id="deco">` and is never interactive. Creatures are
  `<g id="k01" data-type="bee" data-cell="c014">` in `<g id="creatures">`. A creature hidden until
  painting has `data-reveal="c020,c021"` (it becomes visible when all listed cells are painted).
- `page.json`: generated by tools, committed. Contains:
  - `cells`: id, kind, centroid, bounding box, area;
  - `adjacency`: list of neighbour ids per cell;
  - `creatures`: id, type, cell, parameters (for example a bee's number), reveal list;
  - `solution`: BLOOM or GREEN per cell;
  - `stages`: which creatures are visible at each reveal stage;
  - `difficulty`: score and the deduction levels used;
  - `validated`: tool version and date.

**Adjacency rule:** two cells are neighbours if their outlines come within `TOUCH_PX` (start with
6 px at page scale) of each other. Compute it by rasterising each cell as a mask, dilating by
`TOUCH_PX / 2` and testing overlap. Manual overrides live in `page.overrides.json` (add or remove
specific pairs) and win over the computed result. In the app, a long press on a cell highlights its
neighbours, so the player can always see what "touching" means.

## 4. Rules engine and solver

- Rules are pure functions in `src/rules/` used by both the app and the tools, never duplicated.
- A cell state is `BLOOM`, `GREEN` or `UNKNOWN` (v1). A third state `WATER` arrives with the frog
  (later chapter, keep the engine open for it).
- The solver works in **human-like deduction levels**, each level a separate, tested function:
  - **L1 single clue:** a clue whose remaining count equals 0 or equals its unknown neighbours.
  - **L2 pairs:** subset and overlap reasoning between two clues.
  - **L3 global:** the page's total bloom count (shown on the page) and chains of three or more clues.
- A full SAT check (for example the `logic-solver` package, or own backtracking) proves the
  solution is unique. Human-level solving proves it is fair.
- **Staged solving:** solve with the clues visible at stage 0; painting the cells that are now
  determined may reveal new creatures; repeat. A page is valid only if every stage can be progressed
  with L1 to L3 and the final state equals the solution.
- **Difficulty score:** weighted count of deductions by level, plus page size. Report it per page.

## 5. Generator and validator

- `tools/validate <page>`: unique solution, staged no-guess solvability, every cell at least
  `MIN_CELL_PX` across at default zoom and 44 pt at the zoom where it is painted, every creature
  hit box at least 44 pt, all ids consistent between SVG and JSON. Exit code fails CI.
- `tools/generate <page.svg>`: starts from a chosen or random solution, places clue creatures
  (bees mostly, rabbits and foxes as the page allows), checks with the validator, then removes
  clues while the page stays valid, to keep it lean. Hidden-by-paint creatures are placed last.
- `tools/preview <page>`: PNGs of the three states (lines, clues, painted with a default palette)
  into `docs/previews/`, so Kondrat can review on the phone.

## 6. App

Screens: **Title** (continue, chapters, album, settings), **Chapter** (pages with progress),
**Page** (play), **Album** (finished pages, export), **Settings** (sound, hints, left-handed mode,
reset).

Play screen essentials:

- the drawing fills the screen, pinch to zoom, two-finger pan, one-finger pan when not painting;
- a creature list at the bottom: silhouettes with counters (Bees 1 of 3);
- tap a hidden creature to find it; its clue badge appears;
- tools: **Mark bloom**, **Mark green**, **Paint**, **Eraser**, **Hint**;
- a wrong mark shows an ink blot on that cell and leaves it unknown; blots are counted in stats only;
- **Hint:** points to the next provable deduction and highlights the clue cells involved; it never
  just fills a cell;
- painting: tap a marked cell with a colour from the palette; flowers and leaves have separate
  palettes; an "auto palette" button paints the rest;
- the page is complete when every cell is marked; painting is optional but needed to reveal hidden
  creatures; the finished page goes to the album as an SVG with its colours.

Accessibility: BLOOM and GREEN marks use patterns (small dots, fine hatch) as well as colour;
clue badges are numbers or icons; text at least 17 pt; works in portrait and landscape.

## 7. Save

`localStorage` in try/catch, one key per page plus an index, with a schema version from day one and
a migration test. Saves: cell marks, colours, found creatures, blots, time spent. Export and import
all progress as JSON. Album entries are stored as SVG strings; export as PNG rendered through canvas.

## 8. Milestones

- **M0 tools:** page format, adjacency, rules, solver with L1 to L3, SAT uniqueness, validator,
  preview, one hand-made test page, CI. Deliverable: a validation report for that page.
- **M1 vertical slice:** 3 pages (small about 60 cells, medium about 150, large about 300),
  creatures bee, rabbit and fox, the full find, deduce and paint loop with one creature revealed by
  painting, hints, blots, album, save. Deliverable: playable on Pages, tested by Kondrat on iPhone
  and iPad.
- **M2 Garden chapter:** 12 pages with a difficulty curve, page of the day, palettes, sounds.
- **M3:** Forest and Night Pond chapters (snail, owl, frog with WATER, firefly), store builds later.
