# Art references

Owner: Kondrat. Maintained by the `artist` agent.

These apps show the feel we want: dense line art, hidden creatures, calm areas to rest the eye.
They are **mood references only**. Take the feeling, never the drawings, shapes, layouts or
characters. Nothing from them may be traced, copied or used as a placeholder (see ART_BRIEF section 9).

| # | App | Link | What to take |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hidden Folks | https://apps.apple.com/ua/app/hidden-folks/id1584550483 | Hand-drawn feel, hidden figures camouflaged in busy scenes, readable at phone size |
| 2 | Hidden Objects: The Journey | https://apps.apple.com/ua/app/hidden-objects-the-journey/id1673457778 | Layered scenes, objects that sit inside the drawing rather than on top of it |
| 3 | Find It: Hidden Objects Game | https://apps.apple.com/ua/app/find-it-hidden-objects-game/id6446468634 | Density and pacing of the search, how the eye moves across a page |

## What this means for Bloomsweeper

- Creatures camouflage with the same stroke as the cells (ART_BRIEF section 2).
- Density is high at the edges and calmer near the focal motif (ART_BRIEF section 4).
- Revealed creatures sit in hatched shading (GDD section 6).
